# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Death recap showing the killer, damage type, damage amount and biome.
- ~33 situational tips covering stamina, food, Rested, status effects
  (wet, freezing, poison, tar, smoke, encumbrance), damage types, combat
  patterns, boss deaths, environmental deaths, and being caught with a tool
  in hand instead of a weapon.
- Tips are tiered by specificity, so a precise explanation always wins over
  generic advice.
- Repeat-killer detection: the recap notices when the same enemy keeps
  killing you.
- Optional multiplayer broadcast so friends running the mod see your death
  recap.
- Optional death log written to `DeathLog.txt`.
- Localized into 28 languages, falling back to English for anything missing.
